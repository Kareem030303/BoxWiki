About Olympic Boxing

Olympic boxing is an amateur form of boxing featured in the Summer Olympic Games. Many professional champions began their careers as Olympic boxers.

Notable Olympic Champions

  • Muhammad Ali (1960)
  • George Foreman (1968)
  • Sugar Ray Leonard (1976)
  • Oscar De La Hoya (1992)
  • Anthony Joshua (2012)

Facts

  • Olympic boxing first appeared in 1904.
  • Women’s boxing became an Olympic sport in 2012.
  • Olympic boxing follows amateur rules.
