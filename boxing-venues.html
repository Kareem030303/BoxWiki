Professional Boxing Rules

Rounds

Professional boxing matches can last up to 12 rounds.

Scoring

Judges usually score fights using the 10-point must system.

Ways To Win

  • Knockout (KO)
  • Technical Knockout (TKO)
  • Decision
  • Disqualification

Common Fouls

  • Low blows
  • Hitting behind the head
  • Holding excessively
  • Hitting after the referee stops the fight
