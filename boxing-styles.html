Different Boxing Styles

Every boxer has a unique way of fighting. Styles are based on a fighter's speed, power, defense, movement, and strategy.

🥊 Out-Boxer

An out-boxer uses distance, movement, and a strong jab to control the fight. They usually rely on speed and timing instead of standing in close exchanges.

Examples: Muhammad Ali, Floyd Mayweather Jr.

🔥 Pressure Fighter

A pressure fighter constantly moves forward, throwing punches and forcing the opponent to fight at their pace.

Examples: Julio César Chávez, Joe Frazier

💥 Slugger

A slugger relies on power and looks for big punches that can end the fight. They may take more risks to land powerful shots.

Examples: George Foreman, Deontay Wilder

🎯 Counterpuncher

A counterpuncher waits for an opponent to attack and responds with accurate shots.

Examples: Juan Manuel Márquez, Canelo Álvarez

⚡ Boxer-Puncher

A boxer-puncher combines technical boxing skills with strong punching power. They can fight from distance or exchange punches up close.

Examples: Sugar Ray Leonard, Terence Crawford

🛡️ Defensive Boxer

A defensive boxer focuses on avoiding punches through movement, blocking, and timing while looking for opportunities to counter.

Examples: Pernell Whitaker, Floyd Mayweather Jr.

Fighting Style Matchups

  • Out-boxer vs Pressure Fighter: Speed vs aggression
  • Slugger vs Counterpuncher: Power vs timing
  • Boxer-Puncher vs Boxer-Puncher: Skill and adjustments
