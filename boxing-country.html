Countries With Strong Boxing Traditions

🇺🇸 United States

Home to legends such as Muhammad Ali, Mike Tyson, Floyd Mayweather Jr., and Sugar Ray Leonard.

🇲🇽 Mexico

Known for aggressive fighting styles and champions like Julio César Chávez, Canelo Álvarez, and Erik Morales.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

Produced champions including Anthony Joshua, Tyson Fury, Lennox Lewis, and Joe Calzaghe.

🇵🇭 Philippines

Best known for eight-division world champion Manny Pacquiao.

🇯🇵 Japan

A leading nation in the lighter weight divisions, featuring champions such as Naoya Inoue.

🇺🇦 Ukraine

Home to champions like Oleksandr Usyk and the Klitschko brothers.
