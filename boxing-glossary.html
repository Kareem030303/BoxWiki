Boxing Terms

KO (Knockout)

A victory where a boxer cannot continue before the referee's count ends.

TKO (Technical Knockout)

A fight stopped by the referee, doctor, or corner because a boxer cannot safely continue.

Jab

A quick straight punch thrown with the lead hand.

Cross

A powerful straight punch thrown with the rear hand.

Hook

A curved punch thrown from the side.

Uppercut

An upward punch usually aimed at the chin or body.

Southpaw

A boxer who fights with the right hand and right foot forward.

Orthodox

A boxer who fights with the left hand and left foot forward.
